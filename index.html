Delivery checklist

The checks a cut goes through before it is delivered for social or YouTube.

  1. Reframe for each format

    Recompose widescreen (16:9) and vertical (9:16) versions separately, so faces, products and key text stay in frame instead of being cropped by a centre cut.

  2. Place captions in safe areas

    Keep captions and titles clear of each platform's interface, such as buttons and descriptions on vertical feeds, and readable on a phone screen.

  3. Review the audio

    Listen through for clean dialogue, balanced music under voice, no clipping, and no abrupt cuts at the start or end.

  4. Check the export

    Confirm resolution, aspect ratio, frame rate and runtime, then watch the exported file itself before it is sent.
