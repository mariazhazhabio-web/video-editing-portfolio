How a week runs

28 videos in five days, at about an hour each: 40 minutes editing, 10 on captions and post copy, 10 on checks and export.

  1. Monday

    Review last week's numbers (3-second hold, average watch, shares); write or approve scripts with three hook options each.

  2. Tuesday

    Footage in, renamed and logged; frame-rate, HDR and variable-frame-rate issues flagged before editing.

  3. Wednesday

    Edit block one, one client at a time, from that client's template.

  4. Thursday

    Edit block two; review copies with timecode go to clients before 3 pm.

  5. Friday

    Revisions, batch export and quality checks, post copy, then scheduling in each client's time zone.

Delivery checklist

The checks a cut goes through before it is delivered for social or YouTube.

  1. Reframe for each format

    Recompose vertical (9:16), feed (4:5) and widescreen (16:9) versions separately, so faces, products and key text stay in frame instead of being cropped by a centre cut.

  2. Place captions in safe areas

    Keep captions and titles clear of each platform's interface, such as buttons and descriptions on vertical feeds, and proofread them in Australian English.

  3. Review the audio

    Clean dialogue, music ducked under voice, −14 LUFS with true peak below −1 dBTP, no clipping and no dead air.

  4. Check the export

    Confirm resolution, aspect ratio, a constant 25 fps (flicker-safe under Australia's 50 Hz lighting) and runtime, then watch the exported file itself before it is sent.

  5. Clear the music and claims

    Use commercially licensed music on business accounts, and have the client confirm any product or result claim before it goes in a caption.

  6. Label what needs labelling

    Mark paid partnerships with the platform toggle and an “Ad” label, and disclose AI voices or imagery in the post caption.
